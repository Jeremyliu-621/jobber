# Candidate writing style

The reviewed `about me` Google Doc suggests the following writing preferences:

- Use simple, casual, positive, expressive language.
- Commas, semicolons, and colons are natural punctuation choices.
- Longer sentences can be appropriate when they still sound conversational.
- Avoid overly polished corporate language.

These are style signals only. The private life details in that document are not
candidate evidence and must not be used as application facts.

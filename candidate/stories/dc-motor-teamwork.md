Pending candidate review. In a 5-person project to build and explain a DC
motor, Jeremy initially tried to source materials and handle too much of the
work himself. He then broke the work into tasks, matched responsibilities to
teammates’ strengths, shared progress, and set up check-ins. He built and
troubleshot the motor and handled the video explanation while keeping the team
involved. The motor worked, and the stated lesson was to balance empathy with
assertiveness and set expectations earlier.
